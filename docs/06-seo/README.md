# 06 · SEO

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the practice of helping search engines
**find, understand, and rank** your content so the right people can discover it.
Much of good SEO is simply good web development — fast, accessible, well‑
structured, well‑written sites tend to rank well because they genuinely serve
users.

## The mental model

Search engines do three things:

1. **Crawl** — discover your pages by following links and reading your sitemap.
2. **Index** — understand and store what each page is about.
3. **Rank** — decide which pages to show for a query, and in what order.

SEO is about not getting in the way of the first two, and earning the third by
being genuinely useful.

## The honest truth about SEO

> ✅ **Do:** Build fast, accessible, well‑structured sites with genuinely useful
> content, earn real links, and be patient. This is durable SEO.
> ❌ **Don't:** Chase tricks, keyword‑stuff, buy spammy links, or try to
> manipulate rankings. Search engines penalize manipulation, and "black‑hat"
> tactics tend to blow up eventually.

There are no guaranteed rankings and no legitimate shortcuts. Anyone promising
"#1 on Google, guaranteed" is selling snake oil.
